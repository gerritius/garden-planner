spray days page: Many insecticides harm bees: neonicotinoids (e.g. imidacloprid, clothianidin, dinotefuran), pyrethroids and pyrethrins, organophosphates, carbamates, spinosad and fipronil among them. Spray early in the morning or late in the afternoon, never onto open flowers, and not when bees are about.Of your sprays: Pai Benica V spray (pyrethrin — harmful to bees while wet, but breaks down within a day); Spino Ace water granules (spinosad — harmful to bees while wet; safe once dry); Benica X Next spray (clothianidin, permethrin — very harmful to bees, and lasts in the plant (and its flowers) for weeks).
